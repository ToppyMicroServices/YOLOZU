non-overlapping ten-minute job, retains the bounded public artifact for 30 days,

Beads mutation. Missing expected runs are recorded honestly and never backfilled.

## Closure boundary

artifact behavior. They cannot satisfy the Bead requirement for one real
`schedule` event. The scheduling Bead must remain open until that event and its
artifact/issue behavior are read back.
