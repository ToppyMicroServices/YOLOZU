The repeated near-constant segment, clone-to-unique ratios above five on
multiple days, and clone volume relative to repository views meet the documented
automation-sensitive rule. The exact mix of CI, mirrors, scanners, and other
sources is unknown. A GitHub unique cloner is not necessarily a person, so human
adoption from clones remains `unknown`, not 276.
