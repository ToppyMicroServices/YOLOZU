## Observed clone anomaly

The clone series is not credible as human adoption evidence. Most days recorded
roughly 768–822 clones while reporting only 1–4 unique cloners. Examples include:

| UTC date | Clones | Unique cloners |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-07-09 | 822 | 14 |
| 2026-07-10 | 775 | 4 |
| 2026-07-16 | 768 | 1 |
| 2026-07-21 | 768 | 1 |
| 2026-07-22 | 772 | 2 |

This repeated near-constant volume, its clone-to-unique ratio, and the much lower
repository-view count are strong evidence of automation, CI, mirroring, scanning,
or a mixture of those sources. The exact source is unknown. Human adoption from
clones is therefore recorded as `unknown`, not 72.

## Product-page measurement

The public page at <https://www.toppymicros.com/yolozu/> loads Plausible, but no
maintainer-accessible aggregate page-view or YOLOZU outbound-click report was
available during this collection. The page did not expose a public analytics
dashboard. Product-page views and clicks are therefore `unknown`, not zero.

No package telemetry is proposed to fill this gap. A future site-only change may
add aggregate, tagged outbound-click events for the GitHub, PyPI, quickstart, and
completion links, subject to the site privacy policy.

- Record product-page aggregate views and outbound clicks if a privacy-reviewed,
  site-only measurement path becomes available.
- Use the quarterly review to choose `invest`, `maintain`, `narrow`, or
  `deprioritize` from human evidence rather than clone or download totals.

## Baseline decision

The confirmed public distribution and first-run engineering paths are healthy,
but current human adoption is unknown. The evidence supports `maintain and
observe`: improve the stable evaluation onboarding and feedback paths, recruit
design partners, and avoid expanding runtime breadth until human demand is
recorded.
