## Clone automation caveat

The clone series remains unsuitable as human adoption evidence. GitHub reported
324 clones but only 4 repository views across the rolling window. One day also
had a clone-to-unique ratio above five:

| UTC date | Clones | Unique cloners |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-10-05 | 18 | 3 |

These observations meet the documented automation-sensitive rule. The exact
mix of CI, mirrors, scanners, and other sources is unknown. A GitHub unique
cloner is not necessarily a person, so human adoption from clones remains
`unknown`, not 95.

## Snapshot decision

This is the twelfth real weekly snapshot. The four-snapshot target was already
satisfied before its 2026-08-22 acceptance date. Aggregate activity is
measurable, but human adoption and activation remain unknown. Continue the
documented cadence for the quarterly review without treating views, clones, or
downloads as proof of users.
